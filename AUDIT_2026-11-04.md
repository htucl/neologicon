# Neologicon — Multidimensional Audit

**Date:** 2026-11-04 · **Commit audited:** `a7ca616` (75 words × 4 locales, 341 pages) · **Method:** three independent Opus review passes (security/build/infra; frontend/a11y/perf/SEO; usability/content/i18n/product), every load-bearing claim re-verified by hand against the files, the built output, `/usr/share/dict/words`, computed contrast ratios and the GitHub API.

**Deployment status at audit time:** the site is **offline** — no Cloudflare Pages project exists, so nothing below is reachable by the public yet. That is actually convenient: every Critical/High item can be fixed before launch rather than after.

**Standing-rule check (sensitive data):** the content is invented words only; nothing sensitive. A public, login-free deploy is fine *provided only `dist/` is deployed* (see I-3).

---

## Executive summary

The architecture is right for the goal — zero-dependency static build, content as Markdown in git, strict CSP, mobile-first — and the visual design is distinctive. The problems are concentrated in four places:

1. **Input validation is nearly absent in the build** (`parseEntry`), and several fields are interpolated into HTML and even into filesystem paths unescaped. Because word files arrive from strangers' PRs *and* from an automated agent, this is the top fix. One confirmed path: a `category` value of `../../x` writes `index.html` outside `dist/`.
2. **The content has quality defects that undermine the premise.** 13 of 75 headwords are already English words (the first rule in CONTRIBUTING is "must not already be an English word"); ~12 entries use a "noun" as a verb in their own example; one example is copy-pasted between two words.
3. **The daily routine has no guardrails and pushes to an unprotected `main`.** At 10 words/day with the current ~17% real-word hit rate, this will publish roughly 600 dictionary collisions a year, translated three times each, with no review.
4. **The site is not yet a "web app on phones."** No manifest, no apple-touch-icon, light-mode accent fails contrast (3.84:1), search is not a real combobox, and the home page downloads the entire dictionary on every visit (~280 KB gzipped within a year).

Nothing here requires changing the architecture. Most fixes are small; the content clean-up is the largest single job.
