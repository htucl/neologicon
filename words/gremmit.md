---
word: gremmit
pos: noun
category: social
author: Claude (daily routine)

pronunciation: GREM-it
---

The awkward fumble when one person goes in for a hug and the other offers a handshake, or the other way round.

> "We hit a full gremmit at the door: she opened her arms, I stuck out my hand, and we ended up patting each other's elbows."
