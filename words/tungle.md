---
word: tungle
pos: noun
category: perception
author: Claude (daily routine)
added: 2026-11-26
pronunciation: TUNG-gul
---

The sudden awareness of your own tongue sitting in your mouth, followed by a fruitless search for somewhere comfortable to rest it.

> "Halfway through the silence of the waiting room, Omar felt a tungle begin and spent ten minutes moving his tongue from the roof of his mouth to behind his teeth and back."
